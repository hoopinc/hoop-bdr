Project + Context

Premium brands, multi-channel support, one team doing it all

When CX leaders talk with us, we typically hear...

"When you have Sephora, Ulta, Amazon, and DTC customers all contacting support, maintaining consistent brand voice across channels is critical."

"Our AI handles the basics, like where's my order. But for product-specific questions, order issues, and brand-sensitive conversations, the team is still doing everything manually."

"We need something that actually knows our products and policies, not a generic deflection tool."

Moroccan Oil has Ada in place for basic chat (WISMO). It's been there since 2023 and the team seems satisfied enough to keep renewing. The gap: Ada covers the surface-level stuff, but the 6 agents are still handling everything else manually. Product recommendations for professional hair care, order issues across DTC and retail channels, salon vs. consumer inquiries. The CS lead may not know there are better options. Position Hoop as complementary to their current stack, not a replacement for Ada.

Analytics

Ask a question. Get an answer.

No clicking through dashboards to find one number

"What are the top 5 ticket types from our DTC customers vs. retail inquiries?"

"How many tickets last month were product recommendation questions vs. order issues?"

"What percentage of tickets could have been resolved without a human?"

These analytics questions are designed for a CX manager who needs to report up to the COO. If the CS lead can quantify what percentage of tickets are truly repetitive vs. complex, she can make a data-backed case for (or against) expanding automation. The third question is especially powerful: it lets her see the opportunity before committing to anything. Zendesk reporting alone doesn't give you this kind of insight without a lot of manual work.

The "Before" column reflects what we know: 6 agents, Ada for basic chat, everything else manual. The "After" column focuses on what a CX manager cares about: team capacity, quality, and visibility. Note: we're saying "60%+ of routine tickets" not "60% of all tickets." Be precise. The brand voice point matters for a premium brand like Moroccan Oil. Their customers expect a certain standard whether they're buying from Sephora's site or moroccanoil.com. If someone on the team leaves, Hoop absorbs that capacity without needing a replacement hire.
